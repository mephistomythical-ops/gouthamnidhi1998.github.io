A JOURNEY FROM KERALA TO SWEDEN

Grounded in science.
Driven by possibility.

I’m a food systems researcher with a background in biotechnology and a Master’s in Food and Landscape from the Swedish University of Agricultural Sciences.

My experience spans oat-system research at KTH, laboratory research at Lund University, and collaborative work on local food and circular agriculture. I’m interested in the space where scientific evidence meets practical decisions for people and the planet.
