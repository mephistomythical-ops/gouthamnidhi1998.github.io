## Creative coding

Original GLSL implementation for this portfolio, informed by the signed-distance-field and raymarching concepts described in Ben McCormick's Codrops tutorial:
https://tympanus.net/codrops/2024/07/15/how-to-create-a-liquid-raymarching-scene-using-three-js-shading-language/

Creative direction also references the Codrops Creative Hub:
https://tympanus.net/codrops/hub/

No Codrops demo source or third-party JavaScript runtime is bundled. The metal material and motion are artistic approximations, not a physically validated fluid simulation.
